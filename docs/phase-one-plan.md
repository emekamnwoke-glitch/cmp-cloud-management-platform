# Phase One Plan — Chargeback: Insight

> **Portfolio Project** · OrikiTech Consultancy × Aztec Bank Nigeria
> **Rollout Target:** 5 November 2025 · **Status:** ✅ Complete (Close Out 15/12/2025)

---

## 1. Phase Objective

Deliver cost visibility and showback reporting for a scoped set of critical on-premises OpenShift workloads, validating the cost model and building stakeholder trust before chargeback accountability or Phase Two investment was committed.

This phase deliberately excluded enforcement — see [Showback Before Chargeback](../README.md#-showback-before-chargeback) in the README's product decisions.

---

## 2. Scope

**In scope:**
- Infrastructure provisioning for CMP services
- Business unit and application ownership mapping (manual)
- OpenShift Integration Phase I — critical applications only
- Cost allocation configuration and chargeback business intelligence reporting
- IT automation process backend and initial automation workflow implementation
- Stakeholder training and walkthrough

**Out of scope (deferred to Phase Two or later):**
- High availability infrastructure for the CMP platform
- Dynamic/automated service mapping (Phase 1 mapping was manual)
- Full chargeback settlement (Phase 1 delivered showback only)
- Hybrid/multi-cloud workloads

## 4. Exit Criteria

Phase One was gated on the following, all achieved prior to close-out (see [phase-one-closeout.md](../reports/phase-one-closeout.md)):
- Telemetry validated as accurate against known workload cost baselines
- Ownership mapping confirmed by application owners across all in-scope business units
- Showback reports reviewed and accepted by the Finance Team
- Automation workflows tested through infrastructure validation iterations
- Architecture Review sign-off from the EA Team ahead of Phase Two commitment

See the [README Risk Register](../README.md#%EF%B8%8F-risk-register) for the full register.
